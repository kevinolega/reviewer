## **GENERAL CAREER TIPS**

---

### 1. **Start Where You Are**
Don’t wait for perfect conditions.  
Start with the time, skills, and tools you have now.  
Progress beats perfection.

---

### 2. **Stay Employed While Leveling Up**
Keep your current job as your base.  
Use rest days or off-hours to find better work.  
Job hunt in the background. Income keeps you stable.

---

### 3. **Build Skills, Not Excuses**
You don’t need a degree to grow.  
You need practical skills:  
- Communication  
- Customer service  
- Sales  
- Tech support  
- Online tools (Docs, email, CRM)  
Study 15–30 mins daily. Watch. Read. Practice.

---

### 4. **Turn Your Story into Strength**
Use **Hero’s Journey** for success stories:  
- Goal → Struggles → Actions → Success  

Use **Hero’s Tragedy** for failure stories:  
- Goal → Mistake → Lesson → Growth  

Own your background. Make it work for you.

---

### 5. **Fix Your Resume**
Make it short, simple, clear.  
Use Google Docs. Save as PDF.  
No fancy designs.  
No unnecessary info like age, height, or religion.  
Use a clean filename:  
`Firstname Lastname Position YYYY-MM.pdf`

---

### 6. **Apply Like It’s a Numbers Game**
Don’t wait for the “perfect” job post.  
Send out 10–20 resumes a week.  
Apply even if you don’t meet 100% of the qualifications.  
Let the employer decide, not your insecurity.

---

### 7. **Do Great at Your Current Job**
Show up. Be consistent. Help others.  
Good habits now = stronger resume and better referrals later.  
Your next employer will judge you based on how you treat your current job.

---

### 8. **Practice Speaking and Writing Daily**
Take notes.  
Write mock interview answers.  
Explain things on paper.  
Record yourself talking about past work.  
Watch training videos and take notes.  
Improved communication = more job offers.

---

### 9. **Build a Balanced Life**
Stable life = stable worker.  
- Sleep better  
- Eat properly  
- Reduce toxic habits  
- Set goals  
- Be accountable  
Your life outside work affects how you show up in interviews and on the job.

---

### 10. **Never Stop Applying Pressure**
You're not trapped.  
You're just in the middle of your story.  
Keep applying. Keep learning. Keep growing.  
One small step a day will change your life in one year.
