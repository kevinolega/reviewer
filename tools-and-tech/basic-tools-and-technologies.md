# **Basic Tools and Technologies**

Chances are you already know some of these, but here’s a checklist to make sure.  
These tools are essential for job seekers, freelancers, and entry-level professionals.

---

## **Computer Skills**

- ✅ Turn a computer on/off  
- ✅ Use a mouse, keyboard, trackpad  
- ✅ Install and open software  
- ✅ Switch between apps  
- ✅ Type with at least 25–40 WPM

---

## **File Types and Basics**

- ✅ Save and open files (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, TXT)  
- ✅ Rename files properly  
- ✅ Use folders to organize files  
- ✅ Know how to copy, paste, drag, drop  
- ✅ Use cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox)

---

## **Documents**

- ✅ Create and edit documents (Google Docs or Word)  
- ✅ Format text (bold, underline, headings, bullet points)  
- ✅ Save documents as PDF  
- ✅ Use a clean resume template  
- ✅ Create cover letters and letters of intent

---

## **Spreadsheets**

- ✅ Open and read spreadsheets (Google Sheets or Excel)  
- ✅ Enter and sort data  
- ✅ Use basic formulas (SUM, AVERAGE)  
- ✅ Format rows and columns  
- ✅ Track tasks or applications with spreadsheets

---

## **Plain Text Files**

- ✅ Use Notepad or Notes app for writing quick drafts  
- ✅ Write story answers or daily notes  
- ✅ Use for backup if other tools crash

---

## **Web Research**

- ✅ Use Google to search for answers  
- ✅ Use keywords to find job posts, reviews, or how-tos  
- ✅ Spot scams and verify legit sources  
- ✅ Use incognito mode if needed  
- ✅ Bookmark important links

---

## **AI Research Tools**

- ✅ Use ChatGPT or similar tools to ask questions  
- ✅ Ask for feedback on writing or practice interview answers  
- ✅ Use AI to practice English or explain things clearly  
- ✅ Save your best prompts for future use

---

## **Web Communication Tools**

### Google Meet / Zoom

- ✅ Join video calls  
- ✅ Mute/unmute mic  
- ✅ Turn camera on/off  
- ✅ Share your screen  
- ✅ Use chat box for links or comments

---

## **Mobile Tools**

### Notes App
- ✅ Write ideas, to-dos, or reminders  
- ✅ Save useful phrases or sample answers  

### To-Do List
- ✅ Track job applications, study tasks, or errands  
- ✅ Use recurring tasks for daily habits  

### Calendar
- ✅ Set interviews, meetings, follow-ups  
- ✅ Use reminders for important tasks  

### Google Maps
- ✅ Find company locations  
- ✅ Check commute time before interviews  

### Google Docs on Mobile
- ✅ View, edit, or send resumes  
- ✅ Share files quickly during applications  

### OCR (Optical Character Recognition)
- ✅ Use OCR scanner apps to read and copy text from printed papers  
- ✅ Useful for saving flyers, resumes, or IDs into editable format

---

## **Final Tip**

You don’t need to master everything today.  
Go down the list, one skill at a time.  
If you can do 80% of this list, you’re ready to work.
