## **1. Understand the Role**

Sales reps **persuade potential customers** to buy products or services by identifying needs, offering solutions, and closing deals.

### Core Tasks:
- Handle inbound/outbound sales calls  
- Understand product features and benefits  
- Ask the right questions to uncover customer needs  
- Overcome objections  
- Close the sale and meet quotas  
- Log calls and update CRM systems

---

## **2. Learn the Common Tools**

Familiarity with these shows you're ready:

- **CRM**: Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho  
- **Dialers**: Vicidial, Five9  
- **Email/Chat**: Gmail, Outlook, Slack  
- **Documents**: Google Sheets, Docs for tracking leads  
- **Phone Tools**: Softphones, headsets, call loggers

---

## **3. Practice Sales Call Flow**

### Sample Sales Call Outline:
- **Opening**: “Hi, my name is [Name] from [Company]. How are you today?”  
- **Need Discovery**: “Can I ask what challenges you’re facing with your current service?”  
- **Pitch**: “Based on what you told me, here’s how we can help…”  
- **Handle Objections**: “I understand. Many clients felt the same at first, but here’s what changed their mind...”  
- **Close**: “Would you like to go ahead and get started today?”

## **5. Interview Question Practice with Hero’s Journey and Hero’s Tragedy**

### Question: “Tell me about a successful sale you closed.”

**Hero’s Journey Example:**  
> “My goal was to close a high-value sale for a client who said no in the past.  
> I reviewed their history and noticed they didn’t fully understand our package value. I called, built rapport, and asked new questions.  
> They raised concerns about cost and previous delays. I walked them through a tailored solution and explained the ROI.  
> After a 20-minute call, they agreed. It turned into one of my biggest deals that quarter.”

---

### Question: “Tell me about a time you failed to close a sale.”

**Hero’s Tragedy Example:**  
> “I wanted to hit my monthly target and had a lead who seemed ready.  
> I rushed the call and skipped asking key discovery questions. I assumed the client wanted our main package.  
> They said no and went with a competitor who offered something more aligned.  
> I realized I focused too much on selling, not understanding. Since then, I slow down, listen more, and ask better questions.”

---

## **6. Daily Practice Suggestions**

- Practice your pitch out loud daily  
- Record your voice and fix weak points  
- Read product brochures and turn them into customer benefits  
- Practice closing techniques with a friend or mirror  
- Watch real sales calls online and study the structure

---

## **7. Prepare for Metrics Questions**

Be ready to talk about:
- **Quota** – Daily/weekly sales targets  
- **Conversion rate** – How many calls turned into sales  
- **AHT** – Average Handle Time  
- **Retention or upsell stats** (if applicable)
