## **1. Understand the Role**

Tech Support agents solve technical problems over phone, chat, or email.  
Some roles include **transitional sales**, where you upsell or recommend services after solving the issue.

### Core Tasks:
- Troubleshoot devices, apps, or systems  
- Ask probing questions  
- Follow diagnostic steps or scripts  
- Guide the customer through the fix  
- Document the case in a ticketing system  
- Offer upgrades, renewals, or paid solutions (for transitional sales)

---

## **2. Learn Common Tools**

Tech support jobs usually involve:

- **Ticketing systems**: Zendesk, Freshdesk, ServiceNow  
- **Remote support tools**: TeamViewer, AnyDesk, LogMeIn  
- **Knowledge base** platforms  
- **CRM systems** for logging cases and customer info  
- **Basic command-line or system settings** (depending on level)

---

## **3. Practice Tech Support Flow**

### Sample Tech Call Steps:
1. **Greeting**: “Thank you for calling [Company], this is [Name]. How can I assist you today?”  
2. **Confirm the issue**: “So your device won’t turn on, correct?”  
3. **Diagnose**: Ask step-by-step questions  
4. **Guide**: Walk the customer through the solution  
5. **Document**: “I’ll note this in your account...”  
6. **Transitional Sale (if included)**:  
   - “We also have a service plan that covers future repairs. Would you like me to walk you through it?”

## **5. Interview Practice Using Hero’s Journey & Hero’s Tragedy**

### Question: “Tell me about a time you solved a difficult technical issue.”

**Hero’s Journey Example:**  
> “My goal was to help a customer recover data from a crashed laptop.  
> I guided them through basic steps but nothing worked. I looked deeper, found a workaround using safe mode and a USB boot drive.  
> The customer panicked mid-call, but I stayed calm and kept giving simple steps.  
> In the end, we got the files back. They thanked me for being patient, and we got a 5-star CSAT score.”

---

### Question: “Tell me about a time you didn’t solve the issue.”

**Hero’s Tragedy Example:**  
> “A customer had internet issues. I assumed it was a router problem and didn’t dig deeper.  
> After multiple resets and a long call, we found out the main line had physical damage.  
> The customer was frustrated. I didn’t check the big picture soon enough.  
> I learned to always ask broader questions first before jumping to specific fixes. Now I use a checklist and troubleshoot smarter.”

---

### Question: “Have you ever done transitional sales?”

**Hero’s Journey Example (with sales)**:  
> “A customer called for antivirus help. After solving the problem, I mentioned our premium plan with real-time protection.  
> They weren’t sure at first, so I explained the difference and gave examples of past attacks we blocked.  
> They signed up, thanked me for going the extra mile, and we hit our daily sales target.”

## **6. Metrics to Understand and Mention**

- **First Contact Resolution (FCR)**  
- **Average Handle Time (AHT)**  
- **Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)**  
- **Ticket Resolution Rate**  
- **Sales Conversion Rate** (if upselling is part of the job)

## **7. Technical Topics to Review**

- Basic network troubleshooting (modems, routers, DNS)  
- Common PC/Mac errors  
- Mobile OS settings (Android/iOS)  
- Email setup (Outlook/Gmail)  
- Antivirus and system cleanup basics  
- Basic hardware checks (power, cables, device settings)
