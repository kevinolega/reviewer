---

## **1. Set a Clear Target**

- **Goal:** Apply to at least **100 call center companies**.  
- Focus on **entry-level roles**, **customer service**, **tech support**, or **sales accounts**.  
- Don’t wait for job posts — **build a masterlist** of companies and apply directly.

---

## **2. Create a Target Company List**

- Open a spreadsheet. Use these columns:

  | Company Name | Location | Website | Careers Page or Application Link | Facebook Hiring Page | Status (Applied, Interview, Hired, Rejected) | Notes |
  |--------------|----------|---------|----------------------------------|------------------------|----------------------------------------------|-------|

- **Where to find companies:**  
  - Google: “Call center companies in [city/region]”  
  - Facebook: Search “hiring BPO [city]”  
  - Job boards: JobStreet, Indeed, LinkedIn (for leads only)

- Aim to add **5–10 companies per day** until you hit **100 total**.

---

## **3. Research and Organize**

For each company, gather:

- **Location:** Office address or work-from-home tag  
- **Official website:** Find their “Careers” or “Jobs” page  
- **Application link:** Google Form, job portal, or recruitment page  
- **Social media:** Find and follow their FB Page for hiring updates

---

## **4. Prepare Application Requirements**

- **Resume (PDF)** – clean, short, highlights BPO-friendly skills  
- **1x1 or 2x2 photo** – optional, depends on company  
- **Valid ID** – government ID, school ID, or barangay cert  
- **Clear audio setup** – headset with mic for online interviews  
- **Quiet environment** – prepare space for online calls  
- **Mock answers** – practice “Tell me about yourself” and account scenarios

---

## **5. Create a Submission Routine**

- Apply to **5–10 companies per day**.  
- Track your status in the spreadsheet.  
- Mark each status: **Applied, Interview Set, No Reply, Rejected, Hired**

---

## **6. Build Rejection Resistance**

- Expect rejection or no replies.  
- Focus on **consistency**, not outcome.  
- You only need **one yes** — but you may need **99 tries**.

---

## **7. Check and Follow Up**

- Check your email daily.  
- Keep your phone charged.  
- Reply fast to messages or interview invites.  
- If no reply in 1–2 weeks, follow up via email or FB message.
