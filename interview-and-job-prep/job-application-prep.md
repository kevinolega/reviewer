### Step 1: Know What You Want

- **Choose Target Jobs**  
  - Pick 1–3 job types you want to apply for.  
  - Focus on roles you qualify for now or are close to qualifying for.

- **Match Your Skills**  
  - List what you can already do (tools, tasks, soft skills).  
  - Compare your list with job posts.

---

### Step 2: Prepare Your Resume

- **Use a Simple Format**  
  - Clean layout, no fancy designs.  
  - Use bullet points, short sentences, and clear job titles.

- **Tailor for Each Job**  
  - Adjust your resume to match the job title and required skills.  
  - Highlight results (e.g., “Answered 100+ calls per day” or “Reduced wait time by 40%”).

- **Avoid Common Mistakes**  
  - Don’t use “Responsible for…” — use action verbs like “Handled,” “Solved,” “Created.”  
  - Check for grammar and spelling errors.

---

### Step 3: Write a Short Cover Message (if needed)

- **1–2 Short Paragraphs**  
  - State the job you’re applying for.  
  - Mention 1–2 key skills or results that match the job.  
  - Close by saying you're excited to help and available for an interview.

---

### Step 4: Get Documents Ready

- **Resume File**  
  - Save as PDF. Use filename like: Kevin_Olega_Resume.pdf

- **Certificates and IDs**  
  - Prepare scanned copies if the employer asks.

- **Portfolio or Work Samples (if needed)**  
  - For creative, tech, or freelance jobs, have links or files ready.

---

### Step 5: Practice Application Flow

- **Create a Job Tracker**  
  - Note company name, job post link, date applied, and contact details.

- **Practice Filling Out Forms**  
  - Have common answers ready: past jobs, availability, expected salary.

- **Check Your Email Often**  
  - Respond fast to interview invites.  
  - Use a professional email (example: kevinolega@gmail.com).

---

### Optional: Prepare for Online Applications

- **Set Up Accounts**  
  - Update profiles on LinkedIn, Upwork, JobStreet, or local job boards.  
  - Use the same info as your resume.

- **Have a Short Intro Ready**  
  - 2–3 sentences about who you are, what you do, and what job you're looking for.
