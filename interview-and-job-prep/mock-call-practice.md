## **1. Customer Service Mock Call Practice**

### Sample Flow
- Greeting  
  “Thank you for calling [Company Name], this is [Your Name]. How can I help you today?”

- Listen and Confirm  
  “Just to confirm, you're calling about [repeat the issue]?”

- Empathize  
  “I understand how that can be frustrating. Let me help you with that.”

- Action Step  
  “I’ll check that now... Please give me a moment.”

- Update and Close  
  “Thank you for waiting. Here’s what I found... Is there anything else I can help you with?”

### Practice Scenarios
- Late delivery  
- Billing error  
- Account access problem  
- Wrong item received  
- Request for refund

## **2. Tech Support Mock Call Practice**

### Sample Flow
- Greeting and Verification  
  “Thank you for contacting [Company]. My name is [Your Name]. Can I have your name and the device you’re calling about?”

- Listen and Confirm  
  “So your [device] won’t turn on, correct?”

- Ask Probing Questions  
  “When did this start?”  
  “Did you see any error messages?”  
  “Have you already tried restarting?”

- Guide Step-by-Step  
  “Let’s try this: Please hold the power button for 10 seconds... What do you see now?”

- Close and Escalate if Needed  
  “If the issue continues, I’ll escalate this to our Level 2 team. You’ll receive an update in 24 hours.”

### Practice Scenarios
- Device won’t start  
- Internet not working  
- App crashing  
- Error message appearing  
- Password reset issue

## **3. Sales Mock Call Practice**

### Sample Flow
- Introduction  
  “Hi, this is [Your Name] from [Company]. I’m reaching out to help you with [brief value offer]. Do you have a moment?”

- Discovery  
  “Can I ask how you’re currently handling [problem area]?”  
  “What’s the biggest challenge for you right now?”

- Pitch  
  “Based on what you said, I believe [Product] can help by [short benefit statement].”

- Handle Objections  
  “I understand that. Can I ask what’s holding you back from moving forward?”

- Call to Action  
  “Would you like to schedule a demo or try the free version?”

### Practice Scenarios
- Cold calling a prospect  
- Following up after a lead signs up  
- Product recommendation based on needs  
- Handling “Too expensive” objection  
- Closing a sale with urgency

---

## **Tips for All Call Types**

- Speak clearly, slowly, and confidently  
- Avoid filler words: “um,” “ah,” “you know”  
- Practice listening — don’t interrupt  
- Confirm before taking action  
- Stay polite and professional, even if the caller is rude  
- Use call recordings (if available) for self-review
