## **Best Resume Format for Call Center Job Applications**

### Use Google Docs
- We don’t use Microsoft Word anymore.
- Google Docs is easier, autosaves your work, and works on any device.
- You can export your file as a PDF.

### Send Your Resume as a PDF
- PDF keeps the layout consistent.
- Word files can break when opened on other devices.
- To save as PDF:  
  `File > Download > PDF Document (.pdf)`

---

## **Resume Filename Format**

Avoid:
- `Document1.docx`  
- `RESUME.doc`  

Use:
- `Firstname Lastname Position YYYY-MM.pdf`  
  - Example: `Kevin Olega Customer Service Resume 2025-01.pdf`

---

## **Resume Subject Line When Sending via Email**

Bad:
- No subject  
- “Resume”  

Good:
- `Kevin Olega’s Customer Service Resume`  
- `Kevin Olega’s Sales Representative Resume`

---

## **Where to Send Your Resume**

1. Make a list of 100 target call center companies.
2. Visit their websites or Facebook pages.
3. Look for “Careers,” “Jobs,” or “Apply Now” buttons.
4. Email your resume or apply using their form.

---

## **What to Include on a Call Center Resume**

- Full name  
- Professional email (e.g. kevinolega@gmail.com)  
- Active phone number  
- Summary or objective (1–2 lines max)
- Work experience (latest first)  
- Job title, company name, dates, short list of tasks
- Skills section (tools, soft skills, communication)  
- Education (School name, course, years attended)

---

## **What to Avoid Including**

Remove the following:

- Age  
- Birthday  
- Height  
- Weight  
- Religion  
- Race  
- Sex  
- Civil status  
- SSS, TIN, PAG-IBIG, PhilHealth numbers  
- Passport number  
- Signature  
- “I hereby certify…” statement  

---

## **Should You Add a Picture?**

- Optional, but helpful in the Philippines.
- A small 1x1-style photo in the top corner is fine.
- Helps guards/receptionists identify you.

---

## **Keep Your Resume Short and Focused**

- **1-page resume** if you already have experience.
- **2-page resume** if you’re new and want to include school, internships, part-time or freelance jobs, seminars, and skills.
- Don’t include irrelevant or outdated experience unless asked.

---

## **Make It Easy to Read**

- Font: Arial, Calibri, Lato, Helvetica, or Open Sans  
- Font size: 11–14 for body, 16–24 for headers  
- No colors, no columns, no graphics, no tables  
- Avoid using resume builders with complex designs  
- Keep it simple, text-based, and scroll-friendly

---

## **Describe Your Job Experience Clearly**

Bad:  
- “Sales Representative”

Good:  
- “Handled outbound calls to offer insurance services.”  
- “Reached 200+ calls per day. Closed 10–15 sales daily.”  
- “Maintained customer records using CRM tools (Zendesk, Salesforce).”

Use these openers:  
- Responsible for…  
- In charge of…  
- Assisted with…  
- Managed…

---

## **Common Resume Mistakes to Avoid**

- Typos in phone number or email  
- Using unprofessional email (e.g., sexybabe123@yahoo.com)  
- Writing long paragraphs  
- Using Comic Sans or decorative fonts  
- Leaving job descriptions blank  
- Lying on your resume  
- Including unnecessary sections

---

## **How Many Companies Should You Apply To?**

- 10 is too few.  
- 20 to 200 is the real range.  
- Don’t wait. Apply every day.  
- Expect rejection or silence. Focus on volume.

---

## **After You Submit Your Resume**

- Keep your phone nearby at all times.  
- Answer all unknown calls professionally:  
  - “Good morning, this is Kevin speaking.”  
- If you miss a call, call back and send a polite text.  

---

## **Final Reminders**

- Proofread 20+ times  
- Ask a friend to check for errors  
- Print 30 copies for walk-ins (on clean bond paper)  
- Use your resume as a conversation tool  
- Keep a master resume and customize for each job
