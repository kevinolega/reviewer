### Setup

- **Choose a Role to Practice For**  
  - Pick a specific job title.  
  - Review a real job post to guide your answers.

- **Use a Timer or Recorder**  
  - Record your answers or use a stopwatch.  
  - Keep answers under 2 minutes per question.

- **Simulate Real Conditions**  
  - Dress properly.  
  - Sit at a desk.  
  - Use your camera if the interview will be online.

---

### Practice Questions (Common)

- “Tell me about yourself.”  
- “Why do you want to work here?”  
- “What are your strengths and weaknesses?”  
- “Tell me about a time you handled a difficult customer.”  
- “Where do you see yourself in 5 years?”  
- “Why did you leave your last job?”  
- “Describe a time you solved a problem at work.”

---

### Answer Practice Format

- **Use the STAR Method**  
  - **S**ituation – What was going on?  
  - **T**ask – What did you need to do?  
  - **A**ction – What did *you* do?  
  - **R**esult – What happened?

- **Focus on Results**  
  - Mention outcomes: money saved, problems solved, targets hit.  
  - Example: “Helped reduce call wait time from 10 to 3 minutes.”

---

### Self-Evaluation Checklist

- Did I speak clearly and avoid filler words?  
- Did I stay on topic and answer the full question?  
- Did I give a clear example or story?  
- Did I sound confident but not arrogant?  
- Did I smile and look friendly (for video)?  
- Did I avoid complaining about past jobs?

---

### Practice Options

- **Solo Practice**  
  - Read questions out loud.  
  - Record and listen to your answers.

- **Partner Practice**  
  - Ask a friend or coach to roleplay the interviewer.  
  - Give and receive feedback after each question.

- **AI/Tool-Based Practice**  
  - Use mock interview tools or GPT-based bots.  
  - Copy-paste a job post and practice based on it.
