### Communication

- **Speak Clearly and Confidently**  
  - Use simple words and short sentences.  
  - Don’t rush. Pause when needed.  

- **Listen Before You Speak**  
  - Let others finish talking.  
  - Repeat or summarize their points to confirm understanding.  

- **Use a Friendly, Professional Tone**  
  - Avoid sounding robotic or too casual.  
  - Smile while speaking (even on calls) — it changes your tone.

---

### Problem-Solving

- **Stay Calm Under Pressure**  
  - Don’t panic if you don’t know the answer.  
  - Say: “Let me check that for you” instead of guessing.  

- **Look for the Root Cause**  
  - Ask questions to understand the real issue.  
  - Avoid quick fixes that don’t solve the problem long-term.

- **Offer Solutions, Not Excuses**  
  - Focus on what you *can* do.  
  - Example: “Here’s what I can suggest to fix this...”  

---

### Empathy and Emotional Control

- **Show You Care**  
  - Use phrases like: “I understand how frustrating this must be.”  
  - Acknowledge emotions before offering a solution.  

- **Don’t Take Things Personally**  
  - Stay calm even if someone is rude.  
  - Focus on solving the problem, not reacting to their tone.  

- **Match the Customer’s Energy**  
  - If they speak slowly and calmly, do the same.  
  - If they’re rushed, get to the point faster — but stay polite.

---

### Teamwork and Collaboration

- **Be Reliable**  
  - Show up on time. Do what you say.  
  - If you need help, ask early — not last minute.  

- **Share Credit**  
  - Give credit to teammates when talking to clients or managers.  
  - Use “we” instead of “I” for team efforts.

- **Keep Others in the Loop**  
  - Update clients or teammates before they have to ask.  
  - Don’t wait until there’s a problem.

---

### Professionalism

- **Accept Feedback Without Defensiveness**  
  - Say: “Thanks for pointing that out. I’ll adjust.”  
  - Don’t argue or justify unless asked.

- **Take Ownership**  
  - Admit mistakes fast.  
  - Say what you’ll do to fix it.

- **Stay Solution-Focused**  
  - Avoid blaming others.  
  - Keep the focus on moving forward.
