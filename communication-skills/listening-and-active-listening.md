### Interview Situations

- **Show Engagement**
  - Make eye contact.
  - Nod occasionally to signal understanding.
  - Avoid distractions like checking your phone or glancing away too often.

- **Clarify When Needed**
  - Ask follow-up questions if something isn’t clear.
  - Paraphrase the question to confirm understanding: “So you’re asking if I’ve handled tight deadlines before?”

- **Pause Before Responding**
  - Wait a second before replying to ensure the interviewer has finished speaking.
  - Avoid interrupting even if you know the answer right away.

- **Mirror Language**
  - Use keywords the interviewer used in your response.
  - Match tone and formality level to the interview setting.

---

### Customer Service Situations

- **Stay Present**
  - Avoid multitasking during the call or chat.
  - Use the customer’s name when possible.

- **Confirm Understanding**
  - Repeat key concerns: “Just to confirm, you’re having trouble accessing your account?”
  - Ask: “Did I understand that correctly?”

- **Empathize Actively**
  - Say things like “I can see why that would be frustrating” or “Thanks for your patience.”
  - Avoid robotic responses; make it sound real.

- **Avoid Jumping to Solutions Too Fast**
  - Let them finish explaining before offering a fix.
  - Understand the full problem before acting.

---

### Sales Situations

- **Listen for Needs, Not Just Words**
  - Focus on the problem they’re trying to solve, not just what they’re asking about.
  - Take note of emotional cues and repeated concerns.

- **Use the “Talk Less, Sell More” Rule**
  - Let them talk at least 70% of the time.
  - Ask open-ended questions and listen closely to their answers.

- **Reflect and Reframe**
  - Repeat their concern and frame your product as the solution: “You said managing your team is a struggle — here’s how we help with that.”

- **Stay Curious**
  - Don’t assume. Ask: “Can you tell me more about that?” or “What happens when that issue comes up?”

---

### General Tips (Apply to All)

- **Use Verbal Nods**
  - Say: “I see,” “Got it,” “Makes sense” — don’t stay silent too long.
  
- **Avoid Prepping Your Answer While They Talk**
  - Focus fully on what’s being said. Respond only after listening.

- **Take Notes (if allowed)**
  - Helps recall exact phrases and problems.
  - Shows you care about details.

- **Don’t Fake It**
  - Don’t pretend to understand. Ask questions instead.
