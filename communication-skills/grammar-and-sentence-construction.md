How to improve your grammar and sentence construction:

* Spend time writing.
* Writing allows you to see what is wrong with your grammar.
* Read your writing alound after writing.
* Listen to yourself and you'll notice issues.
* Ask for help to get individual sentences checked.

Things to Stop Doing:

* Stop using advanced vocabulary words.
* Stop grouping your sentences into paragraphs.
* Stop using long sentences.
* Stop using compound and complex sentence structures.


Things to Start Doing:

* Use simple words.
* Use solo sentences instead of paragraphs.
* Use short sentences.
* Each sentence should only have one idea.


Things to do less:

* Use less of the following words:
	* Pronouns: Use the specific words instead.
	* Adjectives:
	* Adverbs:
	* Figures of speech:
	

### What do you write about?

* Write about your introduction.
* Write a list of questions you plan to ask at different situations.
* Write detailed step by step instructions about different processes that you know.
* Write about your personal and professional stories.
