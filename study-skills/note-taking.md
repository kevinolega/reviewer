Here’s a simple guide to **note-taking** for call center job applicants — designed for people with limited time, basic skills, or starting from scratch.

---

## **Why Take Notes?**

- Helps you remember important info  
- Makes review faster  
- Turns what you study into your own words  
- Builds writing and communication skills

---

## **What to Take Notes On**

- Common interview questions and your answers  
- Sample spiels for customer service, tech support, and sales  
- Words or phrases you don’t understand  
- Job descriptions and responsibilities  
- Customer problems and how to explain solutions  
- Tips from videos, trainings, and mock calls

---

## **How to Take Notes (Even if You're Not Good at It)**

### 1. **Use Short Bullet Points**
- Avoid full sentences  
- Write what’s important only  
- Example:  
  - *Customer angry – stay calm*  
  - *Confirm issue before giving solution*

### 2. **Use Your Own Words**
- Don’t copy everything word-for-word  
- Rewrite it how *you* would say it

### 3. **Write Questions + Answers**
- Helps when reviewing or practicing  
- Example:  
  - Q: Why did you leave your last job?  
  - A: I was looking for a more stable opportunity with growth.

### 4. **Leave Space for Rewriting**
- Rewrite messy or confusing notes later  
- Rewriting is part of remembering

### 5. **Use Symbols**
- ⭐ = important  
- ❓ = not clear (review later)  
- ✔ = mastered  
- 🔄 = repeat/practice again

---

## **Types of Notes You Can Make**

### Daily Notes
- New words, mistakes, things to fix

### Job Application Notes
- Company name, job post, date applied, response

### Interview Practice Notes
- Story ideas for Hero’s Journey / Hero’s Tragedy  
- Feedback from recordings

### Call Practice Notes
- Sample greetings, objection handling lines, closing spiels  
- Common customer problems + your answers

---

## **Where to Write Notes**

- Notebook  
- Phone notes app  
- Google Docs (autosaves)  
- Flashcards (physical or digital)
