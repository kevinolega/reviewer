# Take Notes

My biggest mistake when I was studying was depending on my memory.  
I forgot important details and lost many opportunities.  
I observed this when I started working.  
Many people stopped taking notes back in school.  
I started taking notes again.

---

## Two Notebooks

I have two notebooks:

- An inexpensive **scratch notebook**  
- A nicer **reference notebook**

Most of my scratch notebooks cost less than ₱30.  
I take 99% of my notes in my scratch notebook.  
At the end of the week, I review and transfer important notes to the reference notebook.

Notes in the scratch notebook don’t need to be organized.  
Write freely and decide later what to keep or discard.

---

## Write Summaries

- Go through your notes.  
- Highlight the most important details.  
- Transfer and organize them on a fresh page or another notebook.  
- My website [https://callcentertrainingtips.com](https://callcentertrainingtips.com) is a summary of all my notes on job applications and call center work.

## Create Your Own Audio Program

My employer is back in school working on his MBA.  
He taught me this secret for studying:

1. Take a lot of notes during classes and study sessions  
2. Highlight important info  
3. Transfer them to a separate notebook  
4. Write summaries  
5. Record yourself reading them aloud  
6. Listen during your commute

Use your phone’s voice recorder.  
Label your recordings clearly with topic and date.  
Example:  
`2022-10-08-Interview-Question-Work-History`

**iOS app suggestion:** Just Press Record

## Create Opportunities: Entertainment to Education

My iPhone shows me how much time I waste:

- 1–2 hours on Facebook  
- 1–2 hours on YouTube/Netflix  
- 2–3 hours on mobile games  
- 1 hour scrolling Instagram  
- 2 hours reading Reddit  

Multiply this weekly, monthly, yearly.

Ask yourself:

- How many hours did I spend on entertainment this week?  
- How long does it take to read one page?  
- What if I watched a 30-minute training video instead?  
- What if I wrote one page a day?  
- 1 page a day = 365 pages/year

**Compare:**

- 20,000 pages of Facebook = no growth  
- 20,000 pages of skills = possibly ₱20,000 more income

If you’re smart, you’ll know what to do next.

## Get Some Rest

You can’t study well if your brain is tired.

**Avoid:**  
- Gaming to relax  
- Scrolling social media  
- Smoking or alcohol for stress

**Do instead:**  
- Sleep  
- Meditate  
- Eat healthy  
- Exercise

A healthy body supports a focused mind.

## Different Notebooks

Use different notebooks for different topics:

- Big sketch pads for planning  
- Standard notebook for work

## Paper and Digital

I use:

- Paper notebooks  
- My computer  
- My smartphone  
- Voice recordings

**Best format?**  
It depends on the moment.

- Paper = fast and offline  
- Phone = quick notes on the go  
- Computer = long-form writing  
- Voice = great while walking/driving

Always review and summarize after.

## Exercise

I’ve battled depression and hopelessness for over 10 years.  
Here’s what helped:  

- Sleep  
- Healthy food  
- Exercise

When I skip workouts:  
- Energy crashes  
- Junk food increases  
- Confidence drops  
- Mood gets worse

**My trick:**  
I hold and swing my 53 lb kettlebell.  
It resets my mood and makes me feel strong again.

---

## Eat More Protein and Less Sugar

Athletes eat more protein — 30–50% per meal.  
They aren’t scared of fat or meat.  
They reduce or cut out sugar and carbs.

When I eat junk, I underperform.  
Try eating clean for 30 days and observe the difference.
